== SUMMARY ==
Dust still veils the ridge and this summary logs a hard week for
the colony. Ash fell on the power array and the water lines. The
food stores held. Our morale dipped after the storm. One directive
closes this report.

== POWER ==
The power grid reads 62 units from the main stack and 30 from the
wind vanes with 8 more held in cells. Crews wiped ash from every
panel at first light. Output is grit-index nominal again.

== WATER ==
The water tanks sit near full after the melt run. A strict ration
rule stays in force at the taps. Filters caught the worst of the
ash and the crew cleaned them out twice.

== FOOD ==
Our food racks came through intact and the yeast vats kept pace.
Each ration bag now carries dried kelp and pressed grain cakes.
Stores will stretch through the cold months if intake stays level.

== MORALE ==
Is morale holding after such a week? Most crews say yes at the
evening count. Did the storm shake the newest hands? A calm shift
rotation and warm meals steady them. The colony sings again in
the mess hall.

== DIRECTIVE ==
The directive for next week is plain. Keep every hazard log current
and post the grit-index nominal reading at both gates so the colony
sees it.
END OF REPORT.
